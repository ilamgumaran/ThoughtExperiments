The Quiet Between Bells

The Quiet Between Bells

A Story About Boredom, Waiting, and the Space Inside a Day

A Resonant Cognition Series Book

Target: Ages 8–11 (3rd–5th grade)

Core concept: Null (Ø) as “nothing tugging right now,” why boredom appears, and how quiet can be safe (not empty).

Illustration: hallway stillness; loose, supportive strings; quiet feels spacious (not lonely).
PAGE 1

Title Page

The Quiet Between Bells

Simple, warm quiet: clock + hallway + bench by a window.
PAGE 2

No Strings Tugging

After learning about Helper Strings, Maya noticed something else.

Sometimes… there were no strings tugging at all.

Slack strings float gently: “nothing tugging right now.”
PAGE 3

Nice… Then Strange

At first, that felt nice.

Then it felt… strange.

Quiet can feel pleasant—and also unfamiliar.
PAGE 4

Early to Class

One morning, Maya arrived early to class.

The room was quiet. The teacher wasn’t there yet.

Quiet classroom: “nothing is wrong,” just early.
PAGE 5

The Brain Whisper

Nothing was wrong.

Nothing needed fixing.

But Maya’s brain whispered, “Do something.”

An insistent nudge: not danger—just “motion needed.”
PAGE 6

The Itchy String

A new string appeared—not tight, but itchy.

It whispered, “This is boring.”

Boredom string: fuzzy/wiggly (itchy) rather than tight or scary.
PAGE 7

Other Kids

Maya looked around.

Other kids were doing their own things:

shuffling papers, tying shoes, staring at the clock.

Each kid has their own “spotlight”: their own inner task.
PAGE 8

Fix It Fast

Maya thought, “Maybe they’re bored too.”

Then her itchy string whispered, “Fix it fast.”
